# Surface: APOD viewer (single-page app)

Mode: Experience. The day's picture leads from the first viewport; the archive, album and game are secondary views in the same world.

Audience and job: curious non-specialists looking at today's sky picture in the evening, people looking up a meaningful date (birthday), returning visitors browsing the archive. Action: look, read the explanation, travel to another day, keep a favourite.

Interview substitution: user said "no questions, finish it in one go"; decision round was not served; assigned direction built unattended with challenger verdicts applied as raises.

Chosen direction: space philately. Every APOD is a stamp issued on its date; the archive is a collection.
Memorable moment: the postmark strikes the stamp when a day arrives, its date wheels rolling digit by digit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each day of the archive is a dated issue in a stamp collection; the date is a postmark you can re-set. Refuses the category default of a full-bleed photo under a glassy black gradient with a starfield, and the cream-editorial opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Stockbook-black album leaf as ground (blue-black, matte). White stamp paper with real perforation teeth framing the picture, a white inscription band in engraved caps (dark ink). Cancellation ink in violet-black; one red for registered/active marks. Archivo (width axis: expanded engraved caps, condensed postmark ring, tabular numerals) plus a text serif for explanations. Controls are labelled engraved-cap tabs; the date control is a canceller with rolling date wheels. Glassine strips hold album stamps.

STORY: The visitor sees today's picture as a giant stamp, reads its catalogue entry, re-sets the postmark to any date or their birthday, flips through a month sheet, keeps stamps in an album, and plays "Smudged Postmark" to guess the year.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One stamp fills the viewport minus a black-leaf gutter: image window ~85% of height, white inscription band below with "ASTRONOMY PICTURE OF THE DAY" in expanded caps, the title large, credit imprint small; day-of-month as the denomination numeral at band right. Circular postmark strikes the band/image corner at right, tied with wavy cancel lines. Primary actions (prev/next day, re-set date, catalogue entry, loupe, album) sit as labelled tabs on the leaf edge.

FORM: Space philately: stamp album and postmark; position 7 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:0ffd263071f55275. Raises: split-flap (date wheels cascade per digit in fixed cells), memory quilt (credit imprint seamed to every stamp), cassette deck (every function a labelled control), fight poster (game reveal splits guess vs truth), alphabet storm (one transformation event per viewport: the strike), zine (reduced motion shows marks already struck).

Signature interaction: the philatelist's loupe, a circular magnifier that reveals the full-resolution image under the pointer. Motion grammar: one strike per arrival (ink presses in with exponential ease-out), digit wheels roll, everything else still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
